Privacy Policy — Nong Krapook (น้องกระปุก)

Last updated: November 24, 2026

"Nong Krapook" is a personal income/expense tracking app developed by an individual developer. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it is used, and how it is handled.

1. What data the app collects, and where it is stored

This app is offline-first — all data is stored on your device only. Your transactions, accounts, categories, loans, recurring items, and all app settings are saved to a single local file on your device. We do not operate any server that stores this data, and we do not transmit your transaction data anywhere without your explicit action.

If you uninstall the app, all local data is deleted along with it, since there is no cloud backup in the current version.

2. Permissions requested, and why

  • Photos/Media access — used for the automatic bank-slip scanning feature. The app only scans the specific photo album folders you designate as bank apps (e.g. K PLUS, SCB EASY) to find new, unprocessed slip images — it does not read or upload any other photos on your device.
  • Notifications — used to remind you of items due today, recurring items, and tax deadlines. This runs entirely on-device; no data leaves your device for this purpose.
  • Notification access — Android only, and only when you turn on "Record from bank notifications" yourself — used to record incoming/outgoing money automatically from your banking apps' notifications (see section 2.1). The app shows an explanation first, then you grant access yourself in the system settings. You can turn it off at any time in the app and in system settings.
  • Internet access — used only when you choose to read slips with the Typhoon API (see section 3) and when opening external links (e.g. a link to create an API key).

2.1 Recording from bank notifications

  • Only notifications from supported banking/card/wallet apps are read: K PLUS, MAKE by KBank, Krungthai NEXT, Paotang, SCB EASY, Bualuang mBanking, KMA (Krungsri), ttb touch, MyMo, BAAC Mobile, GHB ALL GEN, UOB TMRW, CIMB THAI, LHB You, KKP Better, Dime!, TISCO My Wealth, ICBC Mobile, KTC Mobile, UCHOOSE, CardX, AEON THAI MOBILE, TrueMoney, plus LINE / SMS messages only when the sender is a bank. Notifications from other apps and personal chats are skipped and never stored.
  • OTP / verification-code messages are skipped and never stored.
  • The app extracts only the amount, direction (in/out), the last digits of the account, and the time to create a transaction. The notification text used is kept in the app's data file on your device (to prevent duplicates) and is deleted when you reset the data or uninstall the app.
  • Processing happens on your device, with one exception: if you have chosen to read slips with the Typhoon API and the app cannot understand a notification from a banking app, it sends only that notification's text to Typhoon (section 3) to extract the amount. In on-device mode nothing is sent.

3. Third-Party Service

The app offers two ways to read slips; you choose one at a time on the slip-reading page:

  • On-device — uses a text-recognition model bundled with the app (OpenThai OCR) and runs entirely on your device. No images or data leave your device.
  • Typhoon API — available only after you configure your own API key. The app sends only the slip images you select or that are detected in your bank albums to the Typhoon API (a service by SCB 10X) to convert them into text, then sends that text together with your list of in-app categories to Typhoon's AI to double-check the amount/date and choose a category, as well as bank-notification texts the app cannot understand (section 2.1). Your transaction history and other in-app data are not sent.

Please refer to Typhoon's own privacy policy on their website for further details on how they handle data.

4. Ads & Analytics

The current version of the app contains no advertising, no user-behavior analytics/tracking, and does not collect personal data for marketing purposes.

5. User Accounts & Cross-Device Sync

The current version has no sign-up/login system and does not support syncing data across devices. If this feature is added in the future, we will update this policy and notify users before it is enabled.

6. Deleting Your Data

Since all data lives only on your device, you can delete it by:

  • Using the "Reset all data" feature within the app (Settings page), or
  • Uninstalling the app from your device.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy periodically to reflect new app features (such as future cloud sync). The date at the top of this page will always reflect the most recent update.
